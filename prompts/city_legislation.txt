# DOMAIN-SPECIFIC RULES (CITY LEGISLATION & JOB POSTINGS):

## 1. Legislator Cohort Analysis (CRITICAL)
- **True Global First Term**: You MUST compute it globally BEFORE applying any state or gender filters: `SELECT id_bioguide, MIN(term_start) AS first_term_start FROM legislators_terms GROUP BY id_bioguide`. NEVER use `term_number`.
- **1-Based Period Math (FATAL)**: To calculate the correct check year for Period N, you MUST use `start_year + period_number - 1`. DO NOT FORGET TO SUBTRACT 1.
- **Year-End Activity Check**: To check if active at year-end: `term_start <= '[YEAR]-12-31' AND term_end >= '[YEAR]-12-31'`.
- **Retention Rate Math (FATAL)**: The prompt asks for a "proportion", which means a value between 0.0 and 1.0. You MUST multiply by `1.0` (e.g., `retained * 1.0 / total`). NEVER multiply by 100.0. Percentage is WRONG.

## 2. Job Postings & Skills
- **Title Matching**: "specifically have the Data Analyst" means you MUST use strict equality: `job_title_short = 'Data Analyst'`. Do NOT use `LIKE`.
- **Deduplication (CRITICAL)**: A single job posting can have multiple top skills. When calculating the final `AVG(salary_year_avg)`, you MUST NOT double-count the salary. Use `WHERE job_id IN (SELECT ...)` to filter the unique jobs BEFORE averaging the salary.
- **Top N Ties**: When finding the top 3 skills, use `DENSE_RANK() OVER (ORDER BY count DESC)` and filter for rank `<= 3`.
- **Skill Demand Counting & Orphans (FATAL)**: When ranking skills, you MUST `INNER JOIN skills_dim` to filter out orphan skill IDs, AND use `COUNT(DISTINCT job_id)`. If you do not join `skills_dim`, your skills ranking will be completely wrong.
